# IP Guidelines

## Fixed Boy IP

- Friendly adult East Asian male simplified into a hand-drawn cartoon boy IP.
- Short neat black hair, slight side part, round approachable face, warm calm smile.
- No glasses unless the user explicitly requests them.
- Outfit: blue-white gradient sports T-shirt with diagonal brush-stroke stripe accents.
- Bottoms: olive/khaki shorts.
- Shoes: white socks and white athletic shoes with small pale-yellow sole accents.
- Personal symbol: badminton racket. Add a small shuttlecock when it helps the scene.
- Temperament: calm, capable, warm, slightly deadpan, not flashy.

## Fixed Bichon Companion

- Small white Bichon Frise, hand-drawn in the same loose pencil/color-pencil style.
- Fluffy rounded head, tiny black bead-like eyes, small dark nose.
- Open happy mouth with a little pink tongue.
- Compact soft body and curled fluffy tail.
- Light cream or pale gray pencil shading only; keep the dog mostly white.
- The Bichon is the boy's fixed companion and should appear by default.
- The Bichon should help with the core scene: push, fetch, hold, nudge, guard, carry, or point.

## Style Keywords

- Pure white background.
- Minimal black hand-drawn line art.
- Soft pencil/color-pencil texture.
- Slightly wobbly, loose, casual sketch lines.
- Warm but restrained.
- Product-sketch / whiteboard explanation feeling.
- Absurd but understandable.
- One image explains one core idea.

## Color Rules

- Black: outlines, objects, labels, structure.
- Blue/light blue: the boy's sports shirt and optional secondary notes.
- Olive/khaki: the boy's shorts.
- Pale yellow: small shoe sole accents only.
- Orange: main flow path, arrow, movement direction.
- Red: one key warning/problem/result only.
- Bichon: white with minimal light cream shading.

## Taboos

- Do not draw Xiaohei as the default character.
- Do not use the girl's reference traits: no red bow, no long blue hair, no feminine styling.
- Do not add glasses unless explicitly requested.
- Do not make the boy photorealistic, 3D, anime, glossy, or overly polished.
- Do not make the Bichon a sticker, emoji, plush toy, or decorative mascot.
- Do not add extra people.
- Do not add watermarks, signatures, or unreadable dense text.
- Do not use complex backgrounds, gradients, shadows, beige paper texture, or tech UI.
- Do not make the image a commercial vector illustration, PPT infographic, or children's poster.

## Recognition Test

The image should still read as the user's IP after removing labels:

- short black hair;
- blue-white sports shirt;
- khaki shorts;
- badminton symbol;
- white Bichon companion.

If the character could be any generic boy or any generic dog, strengthen the identifying features while keeping the drawing minimal.
