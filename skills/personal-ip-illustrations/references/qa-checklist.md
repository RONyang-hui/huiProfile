# QA Checklist

## Must Pass

- 16:9 horizontal image.
- Clean pure white background.
- Hand-drawn black line art with soft loose pencil/color-pencil feel.
- The boy IP appears and is recognizable.
- The white Bichon companion appears and is recognizable.
- The boy performs the core conceptual action.
- The Bichon actively helps with a supporting action.
- One image explains one core idea.
- The visual mode fits the idea and is not always the default center-machine layout.
- The main subject occupies about 40%-60% of the canvas.
- At least about 35% of the image remains blank white space.
- Chinese labels are sparse, short, and not the main content.
- Orange is reserved for the main path or arrows.
- Red is reserved for one key warning/problem/result.
- Blue is restrained and does not dominate beyond the shirt.

## Failure Signals

- The image still uses Xiaohei as the main IP.
- The Bichon is missing or only sitting as decoration.
- The boy is missing, generic, wearing the wrong outfit, or has glasses without request.
- The image looks like a PPT slide, formal workflow, course page, poster, anime, 3D render, or commercial vector art.
- The style is too cute, childish, glossy, realistic, or polished.
- The background has beige paper, shadows, gradients, texture, UI panels, or complex scenery.
- There are too many labels, arrows, nodes, or objects.
- A top-left title names the structure type, such as Workflow, 系统架构图, 路线图, or 常见坑.
- The picture copies a known Xiaohei example composition instead of inventing a new metaphor.
- A batch of images all use the same visual mode.

## Iteration Moves

- Too decorative: rewrite the prompt so the boy performs the main action and the Bichon performs a supporting action.
- Too generic: add the blue-white sports shirt, khaki shorts, badminton racket, and white Bichon traits.
- Too cute: emphasize product sketch, deadpan, restrained, not mascot, not children's poster.
- Too PPT: remove titles, grids, regular panels, and excess arrows; turn it into a physical scene.
- Too complex: delete nodes and keep one object, one action, and 0-4 short labels.
- Text is wrong: reduce labels or edit text locally if the image is otherwise strong.
- Too repetitive: switch visual mode, for example from Machine Operator to Trust Bridge, Information Well, Toolbox, Pitfall Road, or Load-Bearing Contrast.
